## Read-only code review

`LumiaPixelLayers.cs`, `LumiaLobbyArt.cs`, and the shared `LumiaStoryUI.cs` renderer were also inspected without modification. Lost render targets are rebuilt from the original source texture. Source and output textures retain Point filtering and clamp wrapping. The active render target and `GL.sRGBWrite` state are restored with `try/finally`; the write setting follows the active color space. Story and lobby render targets are released during teardown.

The significant alpha bounds are quantized conservatively to an 80 × 80 logical grid. The lobby's 44 × 66 visible logical cells at integer scale 7 produce a 308 × 462 rectangle at x 780, y 98, with baseline y 560. The native captures confirm this rectangle fits the seat composition and does not enter the menu region.
